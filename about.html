Our story

Built by someone who's been through it.

Hi, I'm Craig. I went through a divorce years ago without knowing a single resource existed — no idea about free legal help, court self-help centers, or forms I could file myself. Now I'm going through another one, and this time I'm finding all of it. I don't want anyone else to have to discover it by accident.

That didn't sit right. The people who need legal help the most are usually the ones going through the worst time of their lives: a divorce, an eviction, an arrest, a custody fight. They're stressed, they're scared, and the last thing they need is to dig through 47 tabs of legalese hoping to stumble onto the right phone number.

So I built The Legal Buffet: one place, every state, every kind of legal trouble — with the good stuff front and center, in plain language, with every link verified against an official source.

What we promise

  • Free, always. No paywalls, no accounts, no upsells. If it costs money, we'll say so upfront.
  • Verified links only. Every organization, phone number, and link is checked against an official source. If we can't verify it, it doesn't go on the site.
  • Plain language. If a fifth-grader can't follow it, we rewrite it.
  • Honest about limits. We're not lawyers and this isn't legal advice — it says so on every page. We'll point you to real attorneys and real aid offices for the rest.

What this site is not

We're not a law firm, we're not lawyers, and nothing here is legal advice. Think of us as the friend who's been through it and hands you the right phone numbers — not the attorney who represents you. For advice about your specific situation, talk to a licensed attorney in your state. We're not a prepaid legal plan, either — those charge a monthly fee for lawyer access, while everything here is free, with nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy.
