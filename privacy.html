Our promise

Privacy

Short version: we don't collect your data. No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking. Here's the full picture in plain language.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms that send us anything. We don't track who visits, what you click, or what you search for on the site. There is no analytics running — we genuinely don't know you're here, and we like it that way.

Cookies

The site sets exactly one cookie: it remembers the language you picked in the translator, so you don't have to re-pick it on every page. That's it. No advertising cookies, no tracking cookies, nothing that follows you around the web.

Third-party services

Two outside services touch this site:

  • Google Translate powers the language picker. When you use it, Google's own privacy policy applies to that interaction — we don't see or control it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses). We never see those logs.

Links to other sites

Every resource link takes you to someone else's website — a court, a legal aid office, a hotline. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we can't speak for them.

If that ever changes

If we ever add anything that collects information — for example, an email newsletter sign-up — this page will say so, plainly, before it goes live. No surprises.
