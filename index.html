Good to know

How to find free or low-cost legal aid

Free help is real

Many people qualify for free or reduced-cost legal help based on income, household size, age, disability, or the type of problem. State legal aid programs, law school clinics, nonprofits, court self-help centers, and pro bono attorneys all offer different kinds of support. Use our state guides to find them — then contact each organization directly, because eligibility and services vary.

Court forms and self-help

Official court websites and self-help centers provide forms, filing instructions, fee-waiver information, and step-by-step guides. They can save you a lot of money — just double-check forms, fees, and deadlines with the court or clerk's office before you file, since rules change.
