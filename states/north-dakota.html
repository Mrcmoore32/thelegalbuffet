How divorce filing works in North Dakota

North Dakota's Legal Self Help Center serves people handling their own civil cases, with a family-law section covering divorce, legal separation, custody, child support, and paternity. Start there before anything else.

Free or low-cost legal help

Legal aid

Legal Services of North Dakota

Free civil legal help for low-income and elderly North Dakotans. Apply at 1-800-634-5263 (under 60) or 1-866-621-9886 (60+).

lsnd.org →
Official state hotline

Report Child Abuse or Neglect

Call the statewide Child Abuse & Neglect Reporting Line at 1-833-958-3500 (TTY 711). In an emergency, call 911.

Community help

Call 211

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

211.org →
If you feel unsafe: call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7), or find North Dakota programs through the ND Domestic & Sexual Violence Coalition.

Key facts — North Dakota divorce

Residency
6 months in North Dakota before starting (or 6 months before judgment)
Waiting period
No mandatory waiting period found in state law
Filing fee
$160 to file, $100 for the other spouse’s answer (per ND court self-help materials)

Source: North Dakota Court System, ndcourts.gov
