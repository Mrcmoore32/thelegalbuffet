Clearing your criminal record in Virginia

Expungement or sealing can hide eligible arrests and convictions from background checks. Here's where Virginia stands.

Availability

Can records be cleared?

yes — expungement for non-convictions, sealing for some convictions since 2025

Eligibility

What qualifies

dismissed/acquitted charges; since July 2025, certain misdemeanor and Class 5/6 felony convictions

Timing

Waiting period

none for dismissals/acquittals; 7 crime-free years for automatic sealing of eligible misdemeanors, 10 years for eligible felonies

Lemon law in Virginia

Bought a new car that's constantly in the shop? Virginia's lemon law may get you a refund or a replacement.

Coverage

Which vehicles qualify

new motor vehicles only

Threshold

When it's legally a lemon

3+ repair attempts for the same defect (1 for a serious safety defect) or 30+ calendar days out of service within 18 months of original delivery

Remedy

What you get

manufacturer must replace or repurchase the vehicle (consumer may choose a refund)

Tenant rights in Virginia

Renting comes with rights most people never hear about until something goes wrong. Here are the key ones in Virginia.

Eviction

Notice before eviction

14-day notice to pay or quit for nonpayment (increased from 5 days effective July 1, 2026); 30 days to end a month-to-month tenancy

Security deposit

Deposit limits & return

Max 2 months' rent; return within 45 days; itemized list required

Repairs

When the landlord won't fix things

May hire a third-party contractor and deduct up to $1,500 or 1 month's rent after 14 days' written notice

Gun laws in Virginia

Gun laws vary a lot from state to state — what's perfectly legal in one state can be a crime in another. Here's the big picture for this state. Laws change often, so double-check anything before you act.

Concealed carry

Carrying a concealed handgun

Concealed handgun permit required; shall-issue (21+)

Open carry

Carrying openly

Generally allowed without a permit (18+)

Buying a gun

Background checks & waiting periods

Private sales: Universal background checks required (restored by 2026 law after a 2025 court ruling struck down the prior version) No waiting period.

Red flag law

Extreme risk protection

Yes. Courts can temporarily order firearms removed from someone found to be a danger.

Out-of-state permits

Reciprocity

Recognizes out-of-state permits, but only for holders age 21 and older

Filming the police: your rights in Virginia

Federal appeals courts across the country have recognized a First Amendment right to photograph and record police officers performing their official duties in public. The U.S. Supreme Court has not ruled on it directly. The right does not protect interfering with officers, trespassing, or breaking other laws while you record.

Audio recording

One-party consent

If you are part of a conversation, you may record it — including with police. (Va. Code Ann. § 19.2-62)

Distance rules

No buffer-zone law

Virginia has no law setting a minimum distance for filming police. You still must not interfere with officers doing their jobs.

Sharpe v. Winterville Police Dept., 59 F.4th 674 (4th Cir. 2023), held that livestreaming a police traffic stop is protected speech, though it did not decide a general right to record. Consent is not needed at all to record conversations in public where there is no reasonable expectation of privacy.
