How divorce filing works in Wisconsin

Wisconsin's court system runs a self-help law center covering divorce, legal separation, custody, and property division — with procedure guides and a guided forms assistant. Start there before anything else.

The official starting point: procedure guides, forms, and a guided interview for divorce and family cases.

Search online anytime, or call (800) 362-9082 / (608) 257-4666 (Mon–Fri, 8–5). Referred attorneys charge no more than $20 for the first 30-minute consultation.

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

If you feel unsafe: call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7), or find Wisconsin programs through End Domestic Abuse Wisconsin.

Key facts — Wisconsin divorce

Residency
6 months in Wisconsin, plus 30 days in the filing county
Waiting period
120 days after service before a final hearing
Filing fee
About $188 in Milwaukee County ($198 with support/maintenance) — varies by county

Sources: State Bar of Wisconsin; Wis. Stat. § 767.335; Milwaukee County fee schedule
