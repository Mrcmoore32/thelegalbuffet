Tenant rights in Connecticut

Renting comes with rights most people never hear about until something goes wrong. Here are the key ones in Connecticut.

3-day notice to quit for nonpayment (after the 9-day grace period); 3-day notice to quit to end a month-to-month tenancy

Filming the police: your rights in Connecticut

Federal appeals courts across the country have recognized a First Amendment right to photograph and record police officers performing their official duties in public. The U.S. Supreme Court has not ruled on it directly. The right does not protect interfering with officers, trespassing, or breaking other laws while you record.

Audio recording

One-party consent

If you are part of a conversation, you may record it — including with police. (Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 53a-187, 53a-189 (criminal); Conn. Gen. Stat. § 52-570d (civil, phone calls))

Distance rules

No buffer-zone law

Connecticut has no law setting a minimum distance for filming police. You still must not interfere with officers doing their jobs.

Criminally it is one-party, but phone calls are all-party by civil law (§ 52-570d), so do not secretly record phone calls. In August 2026 the 2nd Circuit (Massimino v. Waterbury) recognized a First Amendment right to film police in public — binding in Connecticut.

Civil rights violations in Connecticut

If a police officer, employer, landlord, or other authority treats you worse because of your race, sex, disability, religion, or another protected trait, that’s a civil rights violation — and you can report it. Key facts — Connecticut divorce

Residency
At least 12 months in Connecticut immediately before filing or before the divorce becomes final
Waiting period
Traditionally 90 days from the return date — but a 2023 law eliminated the general 90-day wait for noncontested divorces; confirm current rules with the Judicial Branch
Filing fee
$360 entry fee per §52-259 (corroborated by 2025–2026 Superior Court bills of costs)

Sources: CGS §46b-44 (court form JD-FM-177); CGS §46b-67 / 2023 HB 6874; §52-259
