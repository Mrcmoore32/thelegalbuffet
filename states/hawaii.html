Key facts — Hawaii divorce

Residency
6 months in Hawaiʻi (HRS 580-1) — the newest court packet phrases it as domicile at filing with no day minimum, so confirm with the court
Waiting period
No mandatory waiting period; uncontested cases finalize by decree on judicial review
Filing fee
$215 without children / $265 with children (official Fifth Circuit divorce packet PDF)

Sources: HRS 580-1; Fifth Circuit divorce packet
