South Dakota

Divorce, lawyers, and free legal help in South Dakota — verified against official sources.

How divorce filing works in South Dakota

South Dakota's Unified Judicial System runs a legal-help hub with Guide-and-File divorce forms, instructions, and a Legal Form Help Line at 1-855-784-0004. Start there before anything else.

Official court site

SD Unified Judicial System — Get Legal Help

The official starting point: guided divorce forms, instructions, and the Legal Form Help Line.

State bar association

State Bar of South Dakota — Lawyer Referral Service

Searchable directory of South Dakota lawyers, or call (800) 952-2333. Participating lawyers will discuss your issue and their costs with you.

Free or low-cost legal help

Legal aid

South Dakota Legal Aid Offices

Routed through the court's official legal-help hub: East River Legal Services (605) 336-9230 / 800-952-3015, Dakota Plains Legal Services (605) 856-4444, and the State Bar's Access to Justice (855) 287-3510.

Apply via ujslawhelp.sd.gov →
Official state hotline

Report Child Abuse or Neglect

Call 877-244-0864 — intake specialists available Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. After hours, contact local law enforcement.

Housing help

SD Housing — Save My Home

Free one-on-one mortgage default counseling through a network of HUD-approved counselors. Call 1-800-540-4241.

sdhousing.org — save my home →
Community help

Call 211

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

211.org →
If you feel unsafe: call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7), or the SD Network Against Family Violence and Sexual Assault at (833) 203-4307.

Key facts — South Dakota divorce

Residency
Must be a South Dakota resident when the case starts (no minimum duration)
Waiting period
60 days from service of the Summons and Complaint
Filing fee
$97

Sources: SDCL 25-4-30; SD UJS divorce checklist (UJS-307B)
