Divorce, lawyers, and free legal help in Illinois — verified against official sources.

How divorce filing works in Illinois

Illinois courts publish statewide approved divorce forms — accepted by every court in the state — covering divorce with and without children, parenting plans, and support. Start with the court's forms page before anything else.

Community help

Call 211

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

211.org →
If you feel unsafe: call the Illinois Domestic Violence Hotline at (877) 863-6338, or the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7).

Key facts — Illinois divorce

Residency
90 days in Illinois for one spouse (or stationed there in the armed services)
Waiting period
None required by law
Filing fee
Varies by county — check with your county court

Source: 750 ILCS 5/401 (Illinois General Assembly)
