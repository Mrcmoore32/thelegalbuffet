Key facts — Kentucky divorce

Residency
180 days in Kentucky before filing
Waiting period
No decree until the parties have lived apart for 60 days
Filing fee
Varies by county — the AOC raised court filing fees 25% effective July 1, 2026; confirm the current amount with your Circuit Court clerk

Sources: KRS 403.140, 403.170 (cited; Ky. Legislature pages not directly accessible)
