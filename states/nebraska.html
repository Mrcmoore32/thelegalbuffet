Key facts — Nebraska divorce

Residency
1 year in Nebraska (per the official court self-help page)
Waiting period
60 days from service before a hearing can be held (per the official court page)
Filing fee
$200 total "Dissolution Fee" (official Nebraska Judicial Branch fee schedule, effective 07-01-2026, District Courts → Civil)

Sources: Nebraska Judicial Branch self-help page and fee schedule
