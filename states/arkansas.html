Key facts — Arkansas divorce

Residency
60 days in Arkansas before filing, plus 3 full months in the state before a final judgment (A.C.A. §9-12-307)
Waiting period
30 days after filing before the decree can be entered (same statute)
Filing fee
$140 for a new domestic-relations case (Pulaski County Circuit Clerk — a county example; varies by county, confirm with your clerk)

Sources: A.C.A. §9-12-307 (per the AR Circuit Courts Judges' Benchbook); Pulaski County Circuit Clerk
