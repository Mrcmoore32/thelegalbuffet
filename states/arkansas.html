How child support gets calculated — and when it ends — in Arkansas.

How it's figured

income shares (adopted 2020; guidelines on arcourts.gov) Income Shares Chart and worksheets at arcourts.gov

Duration

When it ends

age 18 or high school graduation, whichever is later; if still in HS, until graduation or end of the school year after turning 19, whichever is earlier

Lemon law in Arkansas

Bought a new car that's constantly in the shop? Arkansas's lemon law may get you a refund or a replacement.

Coverage

Which vehicles qualify

new and leased motor vehicles during the quality assurance period (warranty term, up to 2 years/24,000 miles)

Threshold

When it's legally a lemon

3+ repair attempts for the same defect or 30+ calendar days out of service

Remedy

What you get

replacement or full refund (consumer chooses)

Tenant rights in Arkansas

Renting comes with rights most people never hear about until something goes wrong. Here are the key ones in Arkansas.

Eviction

Notice before eviction

30 days to end month-to-month; 3-day notice to quit for nonpayment (civil eviction)

Security deposit

Deposit limits & return

Max 2 months' rent (deposit law applies to landlords with 6+ units or who use a property manager); return within 60 days with a written itemized list

Repairs

When the landlord won't fix things

No repair-and-deduct and no rent withholding; tenant may cancel the rental agreement 30 days after written notice of an unrepaired required issue

Filming the police: your rights in Arkansas

Federal appeals courts across the country have recognized a First Amendment right to photograph and record police officers performing their official duties in public. The U.S. Supreme Court has not ruled on it directly. The right does not protect interfering with officers, trespassing, or breaking other laws while you record.

Audio recording

One-party consent

If you are part of a conversation, you may record it — including with police. (Ark. Code § 5-60-120)

Distance rules

No buffer-zone law

Arkansas has no law setting a minimum distance for filming police. You still must not interfere with officers doing their jobs.

Recording a conversation you are part of is legal with just your own consent. No Arkansas-specific court ruling on filming police was verified; a 2015 right-to-record bill (HB 1669) was proposed but its passage could not be verified.

Key facts — Arkansas divorce

Residency
60 days in Arkansas before filing, plus 3 full months in the state before a final judgment (A.C.A. §9-12-307)
Waiting period
30 days after filing before the decree can be entered (same statute)
Filing fee
$140 for a new domestic-relations case (Pulaski County Circuit Clerk — a county example; varies by county, confirm with your clerk)

Sources: A.C.A. §9-12-307 (per the AR Circuit Courts Judges' Benchbook); Pulaski County Circuit Clerk
