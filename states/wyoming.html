Official state hotline

Report Child Abuse or Neglect

Wyoming has no single statewide hotline — report through your county's DFS office (e.g., Laramie County (307) 777-7921, Natrona County (307) 473-3900, Campbell County (307) 682-7277). Everyone in Wyoming is a mandatory reporter. If it is an emergency, call 911.

dfs.wyo.gov — report abuse →

Key facts — Wyoming divorce

Residency
At least 60 days in Wyoming before filing
Waiting period
At least 20 days after filing before a divorce can be granted
Filing fee
No statewide fee — it is set county by county, so ask your local District Court Clerk (fee waivers available)
