How divorce filing works in Iowa

Iowa's courts provide free interactive divorce forms (with and without children) plus a plain-language overview of Iowa divorce law. Start with the court's self-help page before anything else.

Community help

Call 211

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

211.org →
If you feel unsafe: call Iowa's 24/7 Victim Service Call Center at 1-800-770-1650, text IOWAHELP to 20121, or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233.

Key facts — Iowa divorce

Residency
1 year in Iowa (waived if the other spouse is an Iowa resident and is personally served)
Waiting period
90 days from service before a final decree (can be waived by the court)
Filing fee
$285

Source: Iowa Judicial Branch; Iowa Code §§ 598.5, 598.19
