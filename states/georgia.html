Official state hotline

Report Child Abuse or Neglect

Call 1-855-422-4453 (1-855-GA CHILD) — Georgia's Centralized Intake, 24/7. Phone only; in an emergency call 911.

Community help

Call 211

Free, confidential referrals to local help. Dial 211 from any phone.

211.org →
If you feel unsafe: call Georgia's 24-hour statewide domestic violence hotline at 1-800-334-2836, or the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7).
