How divorce filing works in Oklahoma

Oklahoma has no statewide judiciary divorce self-help page; the official fallback is the Oklahoma Bar Association's free Divorce Information page. Start there before anything else.

Community help

Oklahoma Safe Line

Free, confidential, 24/7 for sexual assault, stalking, relationship violence, and trafficking: 1-800-522-SAFE (1-800-522-7233) — verified on the official oklahoma.gov State Health Department IPV page.

If you feel unsafe: call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (free, confidential, 24/7), or Oklahoma Safe Line at 1-800-522-SAFE (free, confidential, 24/7).

Key facts — Oklahoma divorce

Residency
6 months in Oklahoma (43 O.S. §102; legal sources only — confirm with the court)
Waiting period
10 days after filing (no children) / 90 days after service or entry of appearance (minor children) — per the official OBA divorce page (rev. Aug 2022)
Filing fee
No official statewide figure — confirm with your district court clerk

Sources: 43 O.S. §102; okbar.org divorce page
