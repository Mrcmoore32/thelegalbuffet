Lemon law in West Virginia

Bought a new car that's constantly in the shop? West Virginia's lemon law may get you a refund or a replacement.

Coverage

Which vehicles qualify

new motor vehicles purchased or registered in West Virginia (passenger autos, pickups, vans, motor home chassis)

Threshold

When it's legally a lemon

3+ repair attempts for the same nonconformity (1 for a defect likely to cause death or serious injury) or 30+ calendar days out of service within the warranty term or 1 year of delivery

Remedy

What you get

revocation of acceptance with refund of purchase price (plus tax/fees) or replacement vehicle

Tenant rights in West Virginia

Renting comes with rights most people never hear about until something goes wrong. Here are the key ones in West Virginia.

Eviction

Notice before eviction

Security deposit

Deposit limits & return

No statutory cap; return within 60 days (or 45 days after a new tenant occupies); itemized written statement required

When the landlord won't fix things

No repair-and-deduct statute; courts recognize rent withholding until landlord makes habitability repairs

Filming the police: your rights in West Virginia

Federal appeals courts across the country have recognized a First Amendment right to photograph and record police officers performing their official duties in public. The U.S. Supreme Court has not ruled on it directly. The right does not protect interfering with officers, trespassing, or breaking other laws while you record.

Audio recording

One-party consent

If you are part of a conversation, you may record it — including with police. (W. Va. Code § 62-1D-3)

Distance rules

Buffer-zone law — in effect

30-foot law (W. Va. Code § 61-5-30, Enr. Comm. Sub. for SB 4) — in effect since June 12, 2026; no court ruling on it yet found

After a verbal warning, knowingly and willfully approaching or staying within 30 feet of a first responder while acting to impede/interfere or threaten/menace is a misdemeanor ($50-$500 fine and/or up to 1 year in jail). Sharpe v. Winterville Police Dept. (4th Cir. 2023), which covers West Virginia, held livestreaming a police traffic stop is protected speech.

Key facts — West Virginia divorce

Residency
You or your spouse must have lived in WV at least one year before filing, OR you now live in WV and were married in WV (official Judiciary divorce packet instructions)
Waiting period
No hearing until 20 days after personal service (30 days if served by publication) — per Legal Aid of WV's official self-help divorce materials; confirm with the court
Filing fee
$135 (W. Va. Code § 59-1-11(a)(3), corroborated by county circuit clerk fee schedules)

Sources: W. Va. Code §59-1-11(a)(3); courtswv.gov divorce packet
